# The Perfect Morning Start

A great day usually begins before the first email. My ideal morning combines **movement**, *good coffee* and a simple, nourishing breakfast. It takes about 90 minutes, costs almost nothing and sets the tone for everything that follows.

## What Makes It Perfect

- Moving first: a short run, a bike ride or a stretch session
- Specialty coffee, brewed slowly and enjoyed without a screen
- A plant-based breakfast with real ingredients
- No phone for the first 30 minutes

## My Morning Routine

1. Wake up and drink a glass of water
2. Go for a 30-minute run or a bike ride
3. Brew a pour-over coffee while cooling down
4. Prepare breakfast: oats, fruit, nuts and seeds
5. Sit down and enjoy it before the day starts
